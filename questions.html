About BIP

What is BIP?

BIP: Best Interview Practice is an iPhone app for practicing job interviews out loud. You add the job you're applying for and your resume, choose the interview stages you expect, and answer questions written for that job. When the interview ends, you get coaching on every answer.

Who is BIP for?

Anyone preparing for a job interview, from a first job to senior roles. The questions follow the job's level, and so does the standard your answers are judged against. Early Career is made for people new to formal interviews.

How is BIP different from a general AI chatbot?

BIP runs a structured interview for each stage instead of an open chat: questions planned from the job and your resume, follow-ups like a real interviewer's, scoring against clear criteria, and coaching at the end. It runs on your iPhone, so your answers aren't sent anywhere, and it works offline.

Practicing

Which interview stages can I practice?

Eight: Screening, Competency, Behavioral, Technical, Soft Skills, Leadership, Bar-Raising and Final. You choose the ones your interview process includes, in your own order. Six Explore tools need no job: Warm-up, Resume, Salary, Freelance, Early Career and Retrospective.

Do I have to speak my answers?

No. Speaking is the default, but you can type any answer. Spoken answers are transcribed on your iPhone, and you can listen back, correct the text or record again before you submit.

What kind of feedback do I get?

For every answer: what worked, what to improve next, scores on clear criteria, and how a strong answer is structured, with strong and weak examples. In a mock interview, feedback comes when it ends, never between questions, so practice feels like the real thing.

Can I practice a salary negotiation?

Yes. Salary lets you negotiate base pay, bonuses, commission, equity or overtime with a simulated recruiter who has limits of their own, then shows what moved, what you missed and what to try next time. Freelance does the same for rates, scope and payment with a client.

Privacy

Is my data private?

Your answers, recordings, resumes and results are stored on your iPhone, not on a server. There's no account, no tracking and no ads, and BIP collects no data. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Does BIP send my answers to an AI service?

No. Every answer is evaluated by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to ChatGPT or any other online service.

Does BIP work offline?

Yes. Practice, scoring and feedback all run on your iPhone. You need the internet only to add a job from its link, to subscribe or restore a purchase, and to open these web pages.

iPhone and languages

Which iPhones does BIP work on?

iPhone 15 Pro or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. BIP evaluates answers with Apple's on-device model, so it needs an iPhone that can run it.

Is BIP available on Android or iPad?

No. BIP is made for iPhone.

Which languages does BIP support?

English.

Subscription

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose BIP Premium. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.
