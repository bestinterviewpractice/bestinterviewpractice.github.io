Questions, problems or ideas? Write to bestinterviewpracticeapp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Requirements

BIP needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri). If Apple Intelligence is still downloading, keep the iPhone on Wi-Fi and charging for a few minutes.

Subscriptions

  • To cancel or change your plan, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Bought BIP on another iPhone, or reinstalled it? Open BIP, tap any practice feature, then Restore Purchases.
  • For refunds, use Apple's page: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Everything you add to BIP stays on your iPhone. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
