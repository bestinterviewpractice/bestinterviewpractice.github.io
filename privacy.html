Privacy Policy.

BIP: Best Interview Practice · Last updated: November 2026

BIP helps you practice job interviews on your iPhone. It's built so that your practice stays on your phone. This policy explains what BIP does with your information.

The short version

  • BIP has no accounts, no sign-up and no servers of its own.
  • Your answers, recordings, resumes, jobs and feedback are processed and stored on your iPhone. They can be included in your own device or iCloud backups.
  • Your answers are transcribed and evaluated on your iPhone by Apple's on-device speech recognition and Apple Intelligence. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • BIP contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple.

Information stored on your iPhone

When you use BIP, the following is saved on your iPhone so you can come back to it:

  • the name you choose to be greeted by;
  • resumes you add, after the email addresses and phone numbers BIP detects have been removed (the PDF file itself isn't kept, only its text);
  • job descriptions you add or fetch;
  • your practice interviews: the questions, your typed answers, recordings of your spoken answers, scores and feedback;
  • your settings.

This information is processed and stored locally on your iPhone and is not sent to any server operated by the developer. It can be included in your device backups (for example, iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), depending on your settings; those backups are handled by you and Apple.

You can delete interviews (with their recordings), resumes and jobs inside the app at any time. Deleting BIP from your iPhone removes the data it stored on the phone. It doesn't cancel your subscription (see Purchases and subscriptions below) and doesn't remove copies already in your backups.

Your voice and answers

When you answer by voice, BIP records the audio on your iPhone and transcribes it using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Transcripts of spoken answers are used to evaluate the answer and are not kept; the recording is kept with your interview until you delete it.

Evaluation and feedback are produced by Apple Intelligence running on your iPhone. Your answers are not sent to the developer or to any third-party service.

BIP asks for access to the microphone and to speech recognition only so it can record and transcribe your answers. You can change this at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

Job postings

If you paste a link to a job posting, BIP downloads that web page directly from the website you linked, so it can read the job description. That website receives the request in the normal way any website visit works (for example, it can see your IP address). BIP sends nothing else to it. This is the only time BIP connects to the internet itself, apart from Apple's own services described below.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment and tells BIP only whether you have an active subscription. The developer doesn't receive your payment details. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple's privacy policy applies to these purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

What the developer receives

BIP doesn't collect your interview content, resumes or recordings on any developer-operated server, and it contains no analytics or crash-reporting services. BIP doesn't sell or share personal information.

The developer may receive:

  • emails you send to support, including anything you choose to write in them;
  • information Apple provides to developers, such as App Store sales and subscription reports, aggregated statistics, and crash reports if you've chosen to share them with developers in your iPhone settings.

Children

BIP is intended for people preparing for job interviews and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
